State colors are only for status, never decoration. Against the notch black, they are all bright and high-contrast. Text on orange or blue always uses a dark shade from the same family.

The tone is casual and short: "Done!", "Needs you", "2 tests failed". No jargon when a plain word works.

Motion

Every transition passes through a brief "startle" pose first, so a state change feels like a reaction, not a cut between images. When Reduce Motion is on, all motion becomes a fade.

Size and construction

Notch 22
Panel 44
Roaming 64
Settings 110
  • Head is at least 60% of body height. The silhouette must read at 22 pt.
  • Two oval eyes with a white dot, spaced about one eye-width apart.
  • Pink cheeks are always there. Flat fill, no thick outline, no gradient.
  • While roaming, the robot must never cover buttons or text, and it hides right away during fullscreen or screen share.
  • Sound plays at most once a minute, and can be turned off.
