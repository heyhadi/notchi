Ground rules

Avatar on the left ear, status on the right

The middle stays empty because of the camera. Text only appears when the notch drops into a peek.

Open and close

Hover 300 ms or click to open. Move the mouse away for 600 ms to close. 550 ms spring animation, panel content fades in 200 ms later.

One notch, many agents

The notch always shows the most important state: needs you, then error, done, working, thinking, sleeping.

Safe approvals

Commands always show in full. Risky commands like sudo, rm -rf, or curl | sh get a red label and can only be approved from the terminal.

Macs without a notch

Uses a 200×32 black pill at the top center of the screen, with exactly the same behavior.
